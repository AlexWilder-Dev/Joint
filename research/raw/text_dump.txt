ABOUT THE JOINT
Founded in 2013 as a humble pop-up in Brixton, The Joint was born from a simple obsession: high-quality ingredients and slow-cooked flavors. The crowds spoke, the pop-up stayed, and what started as a local secret quickly evolved into multiple sites.
Over the years, we’ve been proud to receive several awards:
– Winners: Wingfest & Wingjam
– Top 5: Best Ribs in London
– Winner: Best Beef Burger in London
– Top 10: BBQ Restaurants in the UK
You might have spotted us on BBC’s Best Ever Dishes with Tom Kerridge, or read about us in the Evening Standard, The Guardian, and Foodism.
No two Joint locations are the same. We tailor every menu and space to its neighborhood—from Brixton, our ‘OG’ home that is vibrant and authentic, to Greenwich, where you can sip cocktails at our roof bar overlooking the Canary Wharf skyline.
Beyond our permanent spots, you’ll find our food trucks and pop-up setups at the UK’s most iconic events, from the Chelsea Flower Show and Royal Ascot to the front rows of Wilderness and Latitude festivals.
Planning something private? We bring our signature setup to brand launches, weddings, and corporate functions.
The formula is simple: Great food. Local ingredients. Good vibes. We keep the environment relaxed and the quality world-class.”

BRIXTON
Where it all started. Our Brixton Village spot is the true OG. We’re open every single day serving up your favorite brunch, lunch, and dinner alongside a full lineup of hot and cold drinks. Pull up a chair and eat-in with us, or grab it to go via takeaway, click-and-collect, or delivery.

GREENWICH
The Joint Greenwich: More Than Just a Meal. Located right alongside the Greenwich Peninsula Golf Range, we’re the perfect spot to fuel your game or wind down after hitting the greens. Whether you’re playing the full range or a round of mini-golf, join us for everything from breakfast to dinner.
The Best Views: Sip cocktails on our roof terrace or downstairs patio with stunning views of Canary Wharf and the sunset.
