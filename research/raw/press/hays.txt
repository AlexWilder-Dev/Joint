THE VENUE
Welcome to your summer retreat in the heart of London.
Hays on the River returns with a Tanqueray Flor De Sevilla takeover to celebrate the new season!
With riverside views and a brand new look, step inside and unwind with comforting cocktails, hearty The Joint burgers, and our iconic Barrel & Stone pizza - still the heart of our menu. Expect bold flavours, indulgent twists, and perfectly poured drinks to keep the chill at bay.
Whether you’re gathering for drinks in the sun, a celebration, or simply escaping the city-buzz for a moment of calm, Hays on the River is your sanctuary this season. Warm, inviting, and effortlessly stylish.
Our venue is primarily walk in only, however please email haysontheriver@onecircle.co.uk for bookings of 10-15 and exclusive hires.
