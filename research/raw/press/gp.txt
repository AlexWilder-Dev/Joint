The Joint is the perfect spot to hangout overlooking the River Thames and the Greenwich Peninsula Golf Driving Range.
From easy breakfasts to stacked burgers and cocktails, The Joint is all about good
food
and even better company. Expect generous plates, plenty of sides, and a menu made for sharing. Think laid-back, a little messy, and full of those laugh-with-your-friends moments.
Start your day with breakfast, swing by for brunch, or wind down post-golf with dinner and drinks. For something more relaxed, head up to the roof terrace and catch the sunset over the Thames with a cocktail in hand.
Set within the
Greenwich Peninsula Golf Driving Range
, The Joint boasts one of the best spots on the Peninsula, with sweeping views across the river towards Canary Wharf. It’s also just a seven-minute walk from
North Greenwich Underground Station
, making it an easy go-to, any day of the week.
Live music
Ease into your Thursday evenings with live music from
Carla O'Neil
, bringing her laid-back acoustic sound every week from 7:00pm – 10:00pm.
Visit their website to plan your visit.
Access update
Due to ongoing development works on Ordnance Crescent, the usual pedestrian route to the Golf Driving Range, Crazy Putt and The Joint Restaurant & Bar has changed.
From North Greenwich Station
: Please follow signage via Waterview Drive and allow an additional five minutes for your journey.
By car
: Access is via Tunnel Avenue from the south, using the junction with Blackwall Lane.
We apologise for any inconvenience caused. For further information or assistance, please contact The Joint team on 0208 858 7448.
