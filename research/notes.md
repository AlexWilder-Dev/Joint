### Hays Galleria

## Each venue's character, and who goes when

**Brixton: the OG.**
- **The room:** a black corner shopfront on 6th Avenue under Brixton Village's blue iron-and-glass roof. Cream "Brunch.. BBQ.. Bar.." lettering on the fascia, red metal stools outside, white tiles inside that people write on ("a wall of bricks you can write on"), and enamel plates on scrubbed scaffold-board tables.
- **The menus:** typewriter type on paper with black brush-stroke labels, pink section heads and paper-clipped photos.
- **Who goes when:**
  - Weekend brunch crowds and the bottomless brunch (Sun–Fri).
  - Lunch-deal office and market traffic on weekdays.
  - Dinner and drinks with friends; walk-ins pulled in "by the smell of its marinades" (SheerLuxe).
- This is where the awards were won and where the Instagram is shot.

**Greenwich: the roof bar at the golf range.**
- **The setting:** a golf driving range with a roof terrace and downstairs patio, grey cushioned scaffold-board benches, planters, festoon lights, and Canary Wharf to the west for the sunset. The O2 is next door, and The Joint's logo is painted on the glass behind the bar.
- **Who goes when:**
  - Breakfast baps from 8am.
  - Golfers and mini-golfers through the day.
  - After-work groups for the sunset and cocktails.
  - Thursday acoustic nights.
  - Anyone who wants every game on Sky Sports and TNT.
- It is also the biggest space, the natural one for private hire.

**Bermondsey: The Joint as the kitchen inside a bar.**
- **The setting:** you go to Bermondsey Bar & Kitchen and eat The Joint's burgers, wings and dirty fries.
- **What's on:** live music on Fridays, DJs on Saturdays, the match on the big screens, and quiz nights from the bar.
- **Who goes when:** after-work drinkers, match nights and weekend nights.
- **The menu:** the most bar-food of the three, with nachos, pickle pops, deep-fried Oreos and J's hot sauce.

**Hays Galleria: grab-and-go by the river.**
- **The setting:** a burger hut plus a Sweet Hut doing churros and mini pancakes, inside Hays on the River under the Galleria's glass roof, a few minutes from London Bridge.
- **How it works:** mostly walk-in, ordering by QR code from the table.
- **Who goes when:** London Bridge office lunches, riverside drinks after work, tourists.
- No venue photo of Hays or Bermondsey exists in The Joint's library. Their pages reuse Brixton and Greenwich shots.

## What's weak on the current site

1. **Brixton's phone number is wrong.** It has an extra digit, so tap-to-call fails.
2. **No opening hours anywhere,** not even for Greenwich, which has fixed published hours.
3. **Every menu is an image.**
   - Brixton's is a 1654×4680 PNG of about 7MB. It can't be read on a phone without pinch-zoom, can't be searched, and is invisible to screen readers and Google.
   - Greenwich's is three A4 artboards.
4. **Hays shows the wrong menu.** Its page carries Brixton's brunch menu (identical file) and a placeholder image (on the do-not-use list) headed "Brixton… EST. 1994". Hays doesn't do brunch.
5. **Venue pages use other venues' photos.** Bermondsey shows a Brixton photo and Hays shows a Greenwich photo.
6. **The homepage carries another brand's copy.**
   - Honest Burgers' "Honest Insiders" app, "Smash + Grab… Liverpool St", and an "Ultimate Bacon burger until 16 February".
   - Spitalfields and tray images from that brand.
   - "Fint Out More" buttons, and delivery copy pasted from click & collect.
7. **Broken and missing social links.** The site's TikTok link (@thejointlondon) is a dead handle; the live account is @thejointldn. There are no delivery or ordering links even though delivery and click & collect are promised.
8. **The awards are buried** in one paragraph, with no dates or proof. The one verifiable win, Wingfest 2015, isn't specific.
9. **Hire and Christmas lead nowhere useful.**
   - Four "Enquire Now" buttons go to separate generic forms.
   - "London Bridge" appears as a location with no explanation.
   - Christmas "opens 1 November" but there is nothing to register interest.
   - Hays has a "Book a Table" form although it's walk-in.
10. **Link previews are blank.** There are no meta description or Open Graph tags; the title is just "TheJoint".
11. **The media library is full of stock and placeholder files** (Envato stock, about 160 placeholder PNGs, the do-not-use images).

## What the owners most want the site to do (tested against the research)

1. **Send people to the right Joint.**
   - The four places differ more than the name suggests: a market brunch spot, a golf-range roof bar, a bar's kitchen with DJs and screens, and a riverside walk-in hut.
   - Each has different hours, booking rules (Hays is walk-in, Bermondsey books via the bar) and reasons to go tonight (Greenwich's sunset, Thursday music, Bermondsey's DJ Saturdays).
   - The research strongly supports this; the current site actively misdirects (wrong phone, wrong menu, wrong photos).
2. **Turn Christmas, private hire, corporate, food-truck and office delivery into enquiries.**
   - These are the only revenue lines on the Book page.
   - Christmas bookings open 1 November, which is 14 days from today.
   - Instagram's bio leads with "Food truck, festivals, events".
   - Supported.
3. **Make the menus and the awards readable on a phone.**
   - Every menu is an image today, and the strongest proof (Wingfest 2015, Harden's, SheerLuxe) is buried or missing.
   - Supported.
